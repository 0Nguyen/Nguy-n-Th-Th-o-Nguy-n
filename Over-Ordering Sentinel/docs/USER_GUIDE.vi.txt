HUONG DAN SU DUNG

NGON NGU MAC DINH

- Ung dung mo mac dinh bang tieng Anh.
- Ban van co the doi sang tieng Viet o sidebar.

TRUOC KHI CHAY

- Cai Python 3.10 tro len.
- Dam bao `python` co trong PATH.
- Cai dependencies bang `pip install -r requirements.txt`.
- Neu muon dung che do chia se online tam thoi thi may can co internet.

CHAY OFFLINE

1. Bam dup `START_OFFLINE.bat`.
2. Ung dung mo o che do local-only.
3. Khong hien admin.
4. Khong tao link public.
5. Neu launcher offline la ben tu spawn app, dong cua so browser app se dong ca phien offline do.

CHAY ONLINE

1. Bam dup `START_ONLINE.bat`.
2. Mo man hinh admin local tai `http://127.0.0.1:8502`.
3. Neu can thi bat backend app.
4. Dat hoac bo mat khau neu can.
5. Tao link public tam thoi.
6. Gui link cho nguoi test.

CLOUDFLARED

- Launcher online dung Cloudflare Quick Tunnel.
- Neu may da co `cloudflared` trong `tools/` hoac trong PATH thi app dung luon.
- Neu chua co, app se tu thu cai bang `winget`.
- Neu `winget` that bai, app se tai ban Windows AMD64 chinh thuc va luu vao `tools/cloudflared.exe`.
- Neu may khong co internet thi offline van chay, nhung online sharing se khong hoat dong.

LUONG DUNG NHANH

1. Upload file Excel.
2. De Smart Excel Mapper tu tim sheet du lieu va header row.
3. Kiem tra mapping cot tu dong.
4. Neu auto mapping dung thi giu nguyen.
5. Chi sua tay khi can.
6. Xac nhan mapping de chay phan tich.
7. Doc bao cao va tai Excel ket qua.

CHUAN BI FILE EXCEL

- Moi dong nen dai dien cho mot chi dinh, dich vu, thuoc hoac thu thuat.
- Co gang de mot dong header ro rang.
- Han che gop o trong vung du lieu chinh neu co the.
- Neu workbook co nhieu sheet, nen tach rieng sheet README huong dan va giu mot sheet du lieu sach nhat de phan tich.
- Neu duoc, hay ghi gia tri trang thai bao hiem nhat quan trong cung mot workbook.

SMART EXCEL MAPPER

- App co the tu nhan sheet du lieu.
- App co the tu nhan header row.
- App co the tu map cot.
- App cho phep ban noi cot thu cong.
- App co the chuan hoa cac gia tri lien quan den bao hiem ve logic chung cua he thong.

CAC COT TOI THIEU

- `DoctorName`
- `PatientName`
- `HasInsurance`
- `CoveredByInsurance`

CHUAN HOA TU DONG

- Auto mapping hop voi workbook da kha sach.
- Neu preview sau chuan hoa nhin hop ly, thuong nen giu mapping tu dong.
- Neu mot cot trang thai bi toan `unknown`, hay kiem tra lai mapping truoc khi chay phan tich.

NOI COT VA CHUAN HOA THU CONG

- Dung manual mapping khi app chon nham cot hoac bo sot cot bat buoc.
- Dung manual mapping khi benh vien dat ten cot la hoac ma hoa du lieu theo cach rieng.
- Uu tien sua `HasInsurance` va `CoveredByInsurance` truoc vi day la logic cot loi.
- `HasInsurance` la benh nhan co bao hiem hay khong.
- `CoveredByInsurance` la chi dinh cu the do co duoc bao hiem chi tra hay khong.
- Hai y nay khac nhau. Benh nhan co bao hiem van co the co chi dinh ngoai bao hiem.

CACH DOC BAO CAO

- `Overview` va cac the KPI cho biet quy mo du lieu insured, so chi dinh, ty le ngoai bao hiem va tong chi phi ngoai bao hiem.
- `By doctor` dung de so sanh bac si trong cung tap du lieu.
- `By department` dung de xem khoa phong nao co mat do hoac chi phi noi bat.
- `Doctor Red Flag Ranking` la danh sach uu tien xem xet, khong phai ket luan sai pham.
- `Suspicious High-Cost Procedure` tap trung vao dich vu hoac thu thuat chi phi cao can xem ky hon.
- `Required ICD Flags` nhac cac truong hop can doi chieu them ICD hoac chan doan.
- `False Red Flag Context` giup loai bot cac ca nhin do ve thong ke nhung co the giai thich duoc.
- `Case evidence` dua ra vi du dong du lieu cu the de doc sau hon.
- `Tool status` cho biet cong cu nao da chay va co phan nao thieu du lieu.

GHI CHU NGHIEP VU

- App phuc vu review thong ke lien quan den benh nhan co bao hiem.
- App khong tu ket luan gian lan hay sai pham.
- Bao cao dung ngon ngu theo huong can xem xet nhu bat thuong thong ke va can kiem tra.
